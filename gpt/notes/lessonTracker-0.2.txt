Thank you for the screenshot. I stand corrected. You are using EPLAN 
Education Version 2027, which has a modernized ribbon layout compared 
to the older versions. We must use the tabs actually on your screen.

Here are the actual tabs in your EPLAN 2027 interface (from left to right),
and what they are for:

1. File (Green Tab)
   Purpose: Backstage view. Project creation, opening, saving, printing, 
   exporting (like PDF), and global settings.
   
2. Home
   Purpose: The primary editing workspace. Clipboard (Paste), Page 
   Navigator, Text insertion, Properties, Find/Replace, and Graphical 
   preview.
   
3. Devices
   Purpose: Placing and managing devices. Inserting symbols, PLC boxes, 
   terminals, and managing the Device Navigator.
   
4. Connections
   Purpose: Everything related to wires. Connection numbering, cables, 
   potential trackers, and wire routing.
   
5. Tools
   Purpose: Data processing and verification. Project checks (errors), 
   generating reports (BOM, terminal diagrams), and macros.
   
6. Pre-planning
   Purpose: For P&ID-style high-level design before schematic creation. 
   (IGNORE this week).
   
7. Master data
   Purpose: Managing the behind-the-scenes libraries. Symbol libraries, 
   title blocks (plot frames), forms, and the EPLAN Data Portal (parts).
   
8. EPLAN Cloud
   Purpose: Cloud integration. EPLAN eVIEW, cloud projects, and online 
   collaboration. (IGNORE this week).
   
9. Find command (Search Bar)
   Purpose: Not a tab, but the search bar where you can type "Terminal" 
   or "Print" and EPLAN will find the button for you regardless of tab.
