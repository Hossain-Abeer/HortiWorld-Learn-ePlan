1. THE SHORT ANSWER
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- **FA4** (Symbol No. 1195) is a standard 4-pole circuit breaker. It only 
  contains the main power contacts (the 4 poles that carry the load current).
- **FAH4** is a 4-pole circuit breaker **with an auxiliary contact**. The "H" 
  stands for "Hilfskontakt" (German for auxiliary contact). It includes the 4 
  main power poles PLUS an additional, smaller contact used for signaling or 
  control logic.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT IS AN AUXILIARY CONTACT?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
An auxiliary contact is a small switch built into the breaker that is 
mechanically linked to the main breaker mechanism. It does NOT carry the main 
load current. 

According to IEC 60050, it is "a contact included in an auxiliary circuit, 
mechanically operated by the circuit-breaker... e.g. for indicating the 
position of the contacts." (Electropedia, IEV 442-05-30) [reference:0]

In plain engineering terms:
- When the main breaker trips (opens), the auxiliary contact also changes 
  state (e.g., from Normally Closed to Normally Open).
- This allows you to connect a signal lamp, a PLC input, or a monitoring 
  relay to know exactly whether the breaker is ON or OFF (or TRIPPED).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHEN TO USE WHICH?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- **Use FA4** when you just need to protect a circuit and don't care about 
  feeding its status back to a controller or indicator. This is the standard 
  choice for most simple power distribution.
- **Use FAH4** when you need the PLC (or a control panel lamp) to know if the 
  breaker has tripped. For example, if a motor breaker trips, you want to stop 
  the PLC logic and light up a "FAULT" lamp. The auxiliary contact is what 
  gives the PLC that signal.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. SYMBOL LIBRARY REFERENCE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In the IEC_symbol library:
- FA4 is found under: `IEC_Symbol → Beveiligingsschakelaar → 
  Beveiligingsschakelaar, 8 aansluitingen` (Protection switch, 8 connections) 
  [reference:1]. It has 8 connection points (2 per pole for the 4 poles).
- FAH4 will have additional connection points for the auxiliary contact 
  (often 2 more pins for the NO/NC aux contact). The exact symbol number may 
  vary, but the "H" suffix is the visual indicator in the library.
