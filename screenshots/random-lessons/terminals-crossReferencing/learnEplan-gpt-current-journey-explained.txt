1. THE ANALOGY: MUSCLE VS. BRAIN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
To understand this, stop looking at the drawings as "one big circuit" and 
start looking at them as two separate, isolated loops that interact through 
a magnet.

- PAGE 4 (Power Circuit): The Muscle. It uses 400V. It is designed to move 
  the motor. The current here is massive and dangerous.
- PAGE 5 (Control Circuit): The Brain. It uses 230V or 24V. It is designed 
  to make decisions (push buttons, sensors) and send a signal. The current 
  here is tiny.

The current does NOT flow from Page 5 into the motor on Page 4. Instead, the 
current on Page 5 acts as a "remote trigger" to turn the big muscle on and 
off.

2. JOURNEY OF CURRENT: PAGE 4 (THE MUSCLE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Look at your Page 4 drawing:
1. 400V enters through R, Y, B.
2. The current stops at the MCB (Circuit Breaker). It is waiting.
3. The current stops at K1 (Main Contactor). K1 is an open switch. No 
   current can pass.
4. The motor is dead.

THE TRIGGER: The ONLY way the 400V current can move on Page 4 is if K1 closes 
its main contacts. But K1 is a dumb piece of metal; it only closes if 
electricity is applied to its coil. Where is the coil? On Page 5.

3. JOURNEY OF CURRENT: PAGE 5 (THE BRAIN)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Look at your Page 5 drawing:
1. A small control wire taps into Phase R. (It usually goes through a 
   transformer or power supply first to step it down to 230V or 24V, but the 
   logic is the same).
2. The current flows through the OLR auxiliary NC contact. If the motor is 
   cold and healthy, this is closed. Current passes.
3. The current flows through the STOP button. It is NC (Normally Closed), so 
   current passes.
4. The current arrives at the START button. It is NO (Normally Open). It is a 
   dead end. The circuit is broken here.
5. Everything is resting. The current is waiting.

THE ACTION: A human presses the START button.
6. The START button closes. Current rushes through.
7. The current flows into the K1 Coil (A1). It goes through the coil and out 
   to A2, then to Neutral (N).
8. The coil becomes an electromagnet.

4. THE MAGIC LINK (HOW THE TWO PAGES CONNECT)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the most crucial part:
When the K1 coil on Page 5 becomes a magnet, it physically pulls down the K1 
main contacts on Page 4.

9. The 400V current on Page 4 can now flow through K1, through the OLR, and 
   into the motor. The motor starts spinning.
10. Simultaneously, the magnet in K1 also pulls down the K1 auxiliary contact 
    (13-14) on Page 5.
11. The human lets go of the START button (it opens). BUT, the current on Page 
    5 now has a new path: it flows through the closed K1 13-14 contact, 
    bypassing the START button, and keeps the K1 coil energized.
12. The motor keeps running forever, until the STOP button is pressed (which 
    breaks the control circuit on Page 5, dropping the coil, which drops the 
    main contacts on Page 4, killing the motor).

5. SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Page 4 is a 3-phase 400V highway. K1 is the toll booth.
- Page 5 is a 1-phase low-voltage control panel. The K1 coil is the button 
  that opens the toll booth.
- They are physically wired completely separately, but mechanically linked 
  by the contactor's electromagnet.
