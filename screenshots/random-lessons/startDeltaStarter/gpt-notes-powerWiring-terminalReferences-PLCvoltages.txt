1. INTERRUPTION POINTS (CROSS-REFERENCES)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The two images showing `=CA1+EAA/4` and `=CA1+EAA/2` demonstrate EPLAN’s 
automatic cross-referencing using Interruption Points.

- Image 1 (Page 4): The arrows point to `R / 2.9`. This means Phase R 
  continues on Page 2, Row 9.
- Image 2 (Page 2): The arrows point to `R / 4.8`. This means Phase R

reference logic.

2. NEUTRAL (N) VS. EARTH/GROUND (PE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In 3-phase AC power, electricity flows in a loop. 
- The three phases (R, Y, B / L1, L2, L3) are the "push" (Live).
- Neutral (N) is the "return" path back to the transformer. It is tied to 
  Earth (Ground) at the substation, meaning its voltage is roughly 0V.
- Earth/Protective Earth (PE) is purely for safety, providing a low-
  resistance path to trip breakers if a live wire touches a metal casing.

Why consumer electronics don't seem to have it: 
Many consumer devices (phone chargers, laptops) are "double-insulated" and use 
DC power. They only take Live + Neutral (2 prongs on a Europlug) and do not 
need an Earth wire.

Why industrial panels need it:
400V is the voltage *between* two phases (e.g., R to Y). The voltage between 
*one phase and Neutral* (e.g., R to N) is 230V. You must bring Neutral into 
the panel to tap off 230V for control circuits, power supplies, and lighting.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. VOLTAGE TIERS IN AN INDUSTRIAL PANEL
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Your mental model is correct. Panels are divided into distinct voltage tiers:

- 400V 3-Phase (AC): The heavy lifter. Comes from the wall. Drives motors, 
  VFDs (Variable Frequency Drives), and large heaters.
- 230V 1-Phase (AC): Tapped between one Phase and Neutral. Powers contactor 
  coils (sometimes), cabinet cooling fans, and AC power supplies.
- 24V DC: The standard for control logic. Powers PLCs, sensors, actuators, 
  safety relays, and HMIs. Generated by a 230V AC to 24V DC power supply.
- 12V DC: Extremely rare in industrial panels. Stick to 24V DC as the 
  standard (safer for humans, highly resistant to electrical noise).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. STRUCTURE IDENTIFIERS (CA1, EAA)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- `=CA1` is the Location (System/Plant). Likely "Control Cabinet 1" or 
  "Cell A1".
- `+EAA` is the Function (Subsystem). Likely "Electrical Main Distribution" 
  or similar. 
- Together, `=CA1+EAA` reads as "Location CA1, Function EAA".
